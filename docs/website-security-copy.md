# Security & Privacy for Law Firms

**Part 1 — Short website copy**

## How We Protect Your Data

Your firm's contracts and data deserve the same care you give your clients. LegalAI is built on a secure, multi-tenant platform: we share infrastructure for reliability and scale, but your data is strictly isolated. Every record—contracts, analyses, users—is tagged to your firm and protected by access controls at the database level. Other firms cannot see, modify, or export your data.

All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Every connection uses TLS (HTTPS), and your files and records are stored with industry-standard encryption. You never get direct database access—you interact only through the LegalAI web application and exports you request. That keeps things simple and secure.

We log sign-ins, uploads, and document reviews for transparency and audit support. If you ever need to leave, we'll provide a structured export of your data so you can migrate without lock-in. Your data stays yours.
